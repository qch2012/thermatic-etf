Holdings overlap

Overlap of two funds = the sum, over companies both hold, of the smaller of the two weights. It uses each issuer's full holdings file, because a top-10 list makes overlap look too low. Different listings of one company (for example a London share and its US ADR) count as the same company, because they are the same business. Cash and derivatives are left out. Funds without an issuer file show "full holdings unavailable".
